## What runs in this candidate

The velocity/dye/advection/curl/divergence/pressure solver is preserved. Two RGBA16F ping-pong dye banks transport six independent pigment weights so every saved `#RRGGBB` swatch recolors existing dye. The 20 typed controls cover mode, time scale, force, radius, curl, separate velocity/dye decay, pressure retention, shading, color list, pigment/background alpha, background color, color cycle rate, bloom toggle/intensity/threshold, sunrays toggle/weight and ambient rate. The shared `isControlDisabled` hook marks dependent bloom, sunrays, ambient and one-swatch cycle controls inactive without erasing their saved values. Three presets offer quiet draw, source-like motion and bounded live graphite.

The explicit `draw` mode has no autonomous dye. `ambient` mode adds deterministic splats at .05–2 per simulated second; `timeScale` multiplies a host dt capped at 1/30 s without catch-up. The transient `seeded-splats` action adds 1–6 to the current field without Restart or persistence. Its internal queue holds at most 12; each frame applies at most four total drag/manual/ambient splats. `update` never resets the field. Resize or Restart restarts from seed; JSON contains params/seed, not field pixels.

When enabled, real bloom has a source-derived threshold/soft-knee prefilter and six downsample levels; sunrays use source-derived mask, 16 radial samples and two blur passes. The bloom levels are weighted during display in place of the source's additive float upsample. This is a visible adaptation and avoids depending on `EXT_float_blend`. The source demo's `LDR_LLL1_0.png` is absent; display uses original static procedural dithering. Output is borrowed RGBA8, `display-srgb`, opaque or premultiplied according to background alpha. OGL owns all draw state; native GL only checks capabilities/FBO/link and frees this pass's resources. No adapter canvas, Renderer, RAF, listeners, browser storage, external image or dependency.

## Resource policy and evidence boundary

`plan()` accounts for **20** owned color targets: RG16F velocity×2, R16F scalar×4, RGBA16F dye×4, RGBA8 output×1, RGBA16F bloom×6 and R16F sunray×3. Attachment bytes are computed before allocation, capped at the lesser of host lease and 28 MiB; driver overhead is additional. Economy/balanced/detail change only runtime resolution and pressure iterations, never saved recipe. Base with balanced/detail quality is 28 solver passes (including two dye-bank advections) plus one display; optional bloom adds six, sunrays four, and each splat three. Four splats are the regular frame cap. The host re-plans/re-leases and recreates the pass if pixel growth exceeds its original reservation; this pass rejects growth beyond that lease.
